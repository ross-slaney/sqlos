## 6. operator: deeper offset pages are rejected
> GET /sqlos/admin/auth/api/users?page=2
> X-BehaviorLock-Operator: behavior-lock-operator
< 400 Bad Request
< Content-Type: application/json; charset=utf-8
    {
      "error": "invalid_cursor",
      "message": "Offset pagination is no longer supported. Omit page or pass page=1 for the first window, then follow nextCursor."
    }
